Firebase product-category predefined roles

These roles grant full read/write or read-only access to groups of products. They are structured around Google Analytics and general product categories.

Assign these product-category roles to project members using the Firebase console or the Google Cloud console.

Note: If you want more granular predefined roles for your project members, you can assign product-level roles.
